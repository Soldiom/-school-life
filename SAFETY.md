# Child safety and learning safety

School Life uses a safety-by-design boundary for this open prototype.

## Current safeguards

- Friends are fictional characters, not discoverable real users.
- Social actions are predefined positive choices; there is no open messaging.
- There are no public profiles, uploads, comments, leaderboards, or location features.
- There are no advertisements, loot boxes, payments, or paid progression.
- Play sparks never block classes or learning.
- There is no generative AI in the learner experience.
- Learner state remains on the device and can be reset locally.
- Wrong answers receive a hint and an explanation rather than punishment.

## Learning-content status

The included material is seed content for demonstrating the platform. It spans wide age bands and has automated integrity checks, but it has not been certified against every national curriculum or independently reviewed for every supported learner group.

Formal deployments should add:

1. named subject-matter and safeguarding reviewers;
2. local standards mapping and age/reading-level review;
3. bias, cultural relevance, special-education, and accessibility review;
4. a content correction, versioning, and retirement process;
5. human usability testing with appropriate consent and supervision; and
6. a clear route for learners, guardians, and educators to report concerns.

## Features that require a new safety review

Real-user communication, class rosters, uploads, voice recording, location, behavioral analytics, generative AI, purchases, and external links inside learner activities are outside the current safety boundary.

If any of those features are introduced, default settings should protect the learner, adults should receive appropriate controls, and the design should be tested for foreseeable misuse before release.

## Reporting

Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Content or accessibility concerns may be reported in GitHub issues, but never include a child's personal information or private learning record.
